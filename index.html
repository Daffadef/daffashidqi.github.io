Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Telkom Indonesia manages fixed-broadband residential subscribers across DKI Jakarta. The portfolio faced a 10.00% churn rate, risking over Rp500M in vulnerable monthly recurring revenue. Using subscriber telemetry, contract distribution, and CX ticketing data, an intelligence framework was built to uncover churn drivers and field bottlenecks.

Key Analytical Insights

  • Contract Structure Risk: Subscribers on short-term monthly contracts churn at 12.36% compared to just 3.07% on 12-month commitments.
  • Root Cause of Cancellations: Network quality was identified as the #1 reason for churn (~869 users lost), surpassing price sensitivity and competitor competition.
  • Operational Hotspots: Over 4,200 complaints were concentrated in Cakung (2,138) and Ciracas (2,074), where repair times averaged between 4.11 and 4.71 days.

3-Pillar Strategic Roadmap

  • Commercial: Migrate short-term subscribers to 6M/12M contracts via targeted speed upgrade incentives, cutting monthly churn down to 5%.
  • Technical Quality: Repair damaged physical cables and upgrade failing nodes to reduce recurring intermittent disconnects by 25%.
  • Field Operations: Reallocate technician dispatch teams directly to Cakung and Ciracas to bring resolution times down to under 3.0 days.
